# Classroom & study hall

Refresh the office and choose **Classroom & study hall** in the elevator. This is a shared native floor, independent of GitHub projects. It includes nine classroom desks, a teacher's desk, a whiteboard, a subject library, two reading seats and a backpack station. Pip explains these tools too.

Press **E** at visible station controls or use **Class & study tools**. Professor Ellis presents saved lectures on the whiteboard and in a readable panel. Students can work on classwork, save test responses, or raise a hand: Ellis walks from the teacher's desk to their seat. Help uses the selected learning approach and authored lesson guidance. Dismiss him to return him to his desk.

## Curriculum comes next

No curriculum, subject expertise, generated lectures, automated grading or AI tutor is bundled. Add approved lessons with **Add a lesson / test / homework**, then select a lesson. Each has a subject, explanation, classwork, teacher guidance, test questions and homework. Learning preferences save your curriculum name and approach: step by step, visual examples, practice first, or read and reflect. These guide local prompts; they do not train a model.

## Subject library

This library is separate from the break-floor collection. Upload PDF, EPUB, TXT or Markdown, choose a subject section and type (book, article, research paper or reference), then search by subject, title or type. Reading opens at a shared reading seat with notes alongside. Previous and Next turn pages. PDF shows the original page plus extracted selectable text; highlights apply to that text. Scanned PDFs need OCR first. EPUB and text use arranged pages.

Select a passage and click **Highlight selected text**. Notes, highlights and bookmarks persist locally. Use **Save notes**, turn a page, or close the reader to save. Keep backups of important originals.

## Homework and tests

Assign homework from a lesson to place it in the backpack. Save work, submit it for later review, or move it to the break-floor locker. **Homework stored in this locker** at the coat locker retrieves it. Test responses use the same storage. Submission records status; it does not grade answers.

The `study` and `study-library` collections use authenticated facilities storage with revision checks. Individual accounts have separate data; shared-password visitors share the vault. Uploads and records stay outside Git repositories.

## One avatar per browser

Another office tab in the same browser takes control and disconnects the older tab's avatar. The older tab shows **Continue in this tab** to take control back. Separate browser profiles and other people remain visible. Reloading removes stale self representations.
